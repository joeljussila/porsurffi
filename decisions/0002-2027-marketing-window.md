- **Status:** Timeline set by Viljami on 2026-09-26; content, channel permissions and signup mechanics still open.
- **Owner:** Organizers; Oona communications, Julia marketing/visuals, Lauri video and Q setup, Joel overall approval.

## Decision

The marketing push runs **from now through the Q/signup on 2026-10-26 at 07:30 Helsinki time**.
The earlier `ilmo` opening and the later Q/signup are separate milestones:

| Deadline | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| ASAP | Send a deliberately cryptic message in Prodeko TIEDOTUS with the PorSurffi countdown link. The countdown page shows no other trip details. |
| 2026-10-04 | Choose the destination. |
| 2026-10-05 10:00 | Release the promo video. |
| 2026-10-06 12:00 | Countdown reaches zero and `ilmo` opens; publish the full trip announcement and hype. A flight + surf-camp price estimate is required before this. |
| 2026-10-26 07:30 | Q/signup. This is the marketing campaign's conversion moment, not a registration close. |
| 2026-10-27 | Flight deadline. |

Times are Europe/Helsinki. The destination, exact trip dates, final price and capacity are **not**
decided by this scheduling decision. The flight + camp figure is an estimate until real quotes
and terms are checked. The 2026-10-06 12:00 countdown already in `site/index.html` now aligns
with the deadline, but at zero it currently shows only “The reveal is here.” The handoff to
the actual `ilmo` and the live page/domain still need implementation and testing.

## Why the previous alternative lost

The agent's 2026-09-26 draft treated 2026-10-12 as the destination reveal and opening and
2026-10-26 as a registration close. Viljami corrected both: the information/`ilmo` launch is
2026-10-06 and the push continues **toward** the 2026-10-26 Q/signup. The previous calendar
does not represent the organizers' actual funnel.
