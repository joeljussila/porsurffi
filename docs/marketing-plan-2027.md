# PorSurffi 2027 — proposed marketing and audience touchpoint plan

**Working draft, 2026-09-26. Not an approved public calendar.** The 2026-09-24 organizer
meeting agreed on the *sequence*, not the exact launch date: a low-context teaser, a countdown,
a promo video 24–48 hours before opening, then destination reveal and registration together.
The meeting mentioned a Monday 07:00 opening, A3 campus posters after the reveal, and roughly
20 participant places plus seven organizers, but did not confirm the Monday or the cap. See
[meeting context](meetings/2026/2026-09-24-porsurffi-vol-2-meet-1/context.md) and
[current state](../state/trip.yml). The dates below are **proposed organizer targets**, not
meeting decisions or participant promises. Times are Helsinki local time (EEST through
2026-10-25, EET thereafter).

## The launch choice to approve

**Recommend Monday 2026-10-12 at 07:00** for the destination reveal and registration opening.
This leaves a week after the proposed 2026-10-01 destination decision to finish the video,
price, registration rules, website, and creative. The alternative, Monday 2026-10-05, compresses
video review and the 24–48-hour preview into the same weekend as the decision. Moving to
2026-10-19 gives more production time but delays the participant and flight commitment cycle.
Approve or move the entire sequence as one block; do not publish individual dates piecemeal.

The existing `site/` countdown targets **2026-10-06 12:00**. That is inherited site content,
**not** a confirmed launch date, and conflicts with the proposed Monday 07:00 opening. Viljami
must change and test it, and confirm the domain is live, before the first public countdown link.

**Audience and promise:** Prodeko/PoRa students and their networks who want an adventurous,
beginner-welcoming surf trip. The destination is still one of Bali, Guatemala, or El Salvador.
Before the reveal, sell the feeling without accidentally disclosing a destination; at the reveal,
state the chosen destination, exact travel dates, complete price/inclusions, participant cap,
selection/queue rules, payment and cancellation terms, and a working signup link. Do not imply
that flights, camp, budget, or exact travel dates are booked until verified. One canonical trip
page carries the current facts; public posts link there rather than repeating stale details.

## Simple measurement and operating rule

Oona keeps one campaign checklist: each touchpoint's approved copy, asset/link, sender,
scheduled time, live URL, and actual send time. Lauri tracks form submissions and drop-offs
without public applicant identities. Review qualified signups versus the **provisional** 20
places at the 2026-10-19 midpoint, then decide whether to add an approved extra reminder.
For every official participant announcement use one ask and an exact deadline; LÖRS handles
discussion. No agent publishes, contacts the audience, books, or pays on its own.
