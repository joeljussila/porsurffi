## Campaign logic

**Mystery → information → repeated reasons to join → clear Q instructions.** The first
message and countdown page reveal essentially nothing. The promo video arrives on
**Monday 2026-10-05 at 10:00**. At **Tuesday 2026-10-06 at 12:00**, the countdown ends,
the destination and trip information go public, and the `ilmo` opens. This starts the
main marketing push, which continues for almost three weeks until the separate
**Monday 2026-10-26 at 07:30 Q/signup**. Flights have a **Tuesday 2026-10-27** deadline.

Use one canonical, current information page after October 6. The pre-reveal countdown page
should have no other trip details. Do not claim a confirmed price or itinerary based only on
an estimate. Explain the `ilmo` and Q mechanics plainly once organizers settle what each
requires from the audience. No automated or agent-sent audience messages.
