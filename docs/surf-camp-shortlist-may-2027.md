## Village decision for early May

| Country | One base for all nine nights | Group fit and important qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Guatemala | **El Paredón** | Best cluster of surf lodging, food, and social space. May's larger southern swell at the exposed sand-bottom beach can make beginner lessons difficult; strong rips are a hazard. Require a documented daily lesson-safety and poor-surf plan. [Wave guide](https://www.surf-forecast.com/breaks/El-Paredon). |
| El Salvador | **El Tunco / El Sunzal corridor** | El Tunco gives the group a social lodging base; nearby Sunzal is an important coaching option, with other breaks for stronger surfers. Early-May south swell may make individual point breaks too large for novices. Require transport, level-based groups, and a named beginner fallback. [Destination information](https://elsalvador.travel/esp/en/el-sunzal/). |
| Bali | **Canggu / Pererenan** | Early May starts the west-coast-favouring season. More learner options and camp capacity than Uluwatu or Medewi as a sole base for 30 mixed-level guests. Crowded lineups require small lesson groups and daily spot choice. [Surf-season guide](https://kimasurfbali.com/bali-surf-season-all-year-round/). |

**Provisional Porsurf ranking:** Bali first for a 30-person beginner-welcoming trip; El Salvador second if the operator can show a safe May lesson programme; Guatemala third because everyone depends heavily on one exposed beach. This is a trip-design judgement, not a quoted cost comparison. Airfare and 2027 camp availability could change the ranking.

## Original 30-person quote brief (planning reference)

This draft predates outreach and is **not** an audited copy of the emails in Sent. The agent handoff later used a more casual version.

**Subject:** Porsurf group enquiry | 30 guests | 3–12 May 2027

Hello,

I’m organising Porsurf, a social surf trip for a group of 30 travelling from Finland. We’re considering your camp for **check-in on 3 May and check-out on 12 May 2027 (nine nights)**. Many participants will be beginners, with some more experienced surfers. Could you confirm whether you can accommodate all 30 during those exact dates and send a group quote?

Please include:

1. A room-by-room plan showing **30 individual beds** (shared dorms are fine), bathrooms, and whether we could reserve a shared gathering space or an exclusive area.
2. A price for nine nights with breakfast and **five coached surf days** for beginners, plus a separate option for experienced surfers. Please specify the number of instructors, lesson-group sizes, lesson duration, boards, and transport to lesson spots. We would also welcome prices for extra surf days and optional group dinners.
3. Your early-May plan for days when the usual beginner break is too big or crowded, including how you decide whether it is safe to teach and what alternative locations or activities are available.
4. Return airport transfers for 30 people, all taxes and fees, total group price and per-person price, deposit and payment schedule, cancellation terms, and how long an offer could be held.

We’re comparing locations at this stage, so no rooms need to be held yet. Thank you, and I look forward to hearing what you could put together.

Best,
Viljami
