## Program (what they actually ran)

Two surf groups A/B departing 08:30 / 09:30, boards rented all day so people could re-surf in
the evening · yoga · safari (1–1.5 h each way) · **SPP** day trip (06:15 departure, bingo card)
· approt bar crawl with a points task list · **tropical sitsit / white party** · megabileet ·
snorkelling (attempted twice, weather-dependent, one day lost) · Ella scenic train flagged as
overpriced online, buy at the station `[2026-04-13]`.

Rooms were mixed 2-person / 3-person / dorm / villa, assigned by the organizers `[2026-05-05]`.

## What to carry forward

- **Two-channel comms**: `TIEDOTUS` (official, one-way, organizers only) + `LÖRS` (free chat).
  Clean separation, no noise in the official feed. Keep this.
- **Vastuurinki** (responsibility ring) — every one of the 25 named and assigned.
- **Survival Guide** shipped a week out.
- **PreSurf Ilta** got the whole group in one room before departure.
- Daily schedule posted to TIEDOTUS each evening for the next day. Heavy but it worked.

## Friction worth fixing

- **Under-filled by ~5 seats** vs. the 30 target.
- **ETA chasing** — repeated reminders needed `[2026-02-15, 2026-04-09]`; Oona had to track
  who'd been approved by hand and threatened to spam DMs.
- **Shirt sizing** ran through a Forms round with missing responses and a size change that
  forced a re-download `[2026-03-18]`; final count wobbled between 26 and 27 right up to order.
- **Cancellations are expensive.** Two data points on refunds: 1000 € flight → 250 € back;
  800 € flight → 40 € back `[2026-03-31]`. Only taxes are recoverable, and it depends on the
  carrier. Budget for no-shows rather than assuming refunds.
- **Personal bank account** held participant money. Fine at 25 people; worth a thought at 30+.
- Arugam Bay carried a **2024 security advisory** (UK FCDO + US embassy, threat to Israeli
  tourists) that the organizers reviewed before committing `[2025-11-17]`.
