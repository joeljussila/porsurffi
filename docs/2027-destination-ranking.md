# PorSurffi 2027 — scored ranking

Companion to [`2027-destination-analysis.md`](2027-destination-analysis.md). Prepared 2026-09-21.

## How to read this

**Scored on five criteria**, weighted by how much they actually affect the trip:

| Criterion | Weight | What it measures |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Waves in May for *your* level** | 35 % | 0–50 sessions, avg ~7. Sand > reef. Needs push, not mush. |
| **Cost** (flights + camp only) | 25 % | Per person, as you asked — nothing else included. |
| **Group logistics** | 20 % | Can one supplier take ~30 with instructors throughout? |
| **Non-surf activities** | 10 % | Within a 2–4 h drive. The 2026 safari/jungle equivalent. |
| **Flight practicality** | 10 % | Route depth, checked baggage, transit visas. |

**Prices are estimates, not quotes.** Every figure links to its source so you can verify.

> **Important: these are retail per-person rates.** Your 2026 surf camp cost **370 USD ≈ €310
> for 8 nights — about €39/night**, which is far below anything advertised below. That was a
> negotiated group rate. **Expect 20–40 % off retail for a 30-person block.** If you read the
> ranges below as what you'll pay, you'll conclude the trip is unaffordable. It isn't — you just
> have to ask.

**Cost basis:** return flight from HEL **including 23 kg checked baggage**, plus camp
accommodation + lessons for **9–11 nights**. Excludes food beyond what camps include, visas,
transfers, merch, program.

### What the ranking actually says

**Bali tops the table — and the reason to reject it is a preference, not a defect.** It is cheap
to reach, has enormous group capacity, great activities and genuinely good May surf. Nothing is
wrong with it except that it's the obvious answer, which you've told me twice you don't want.
That's a legitimate reason, but be clear it's a *taste* decision costing you roughly €150–250 per
person and some logistical ease.

**Lombok is the interesting answer: Bali's advantages without being Bali.** Same region, same
flights, same visa, one short hop further — and a *better* wave setup for a group with a 50-session
spread.

**El Salvador has the best waves and the worst logistics.** It scores highest on the thing that
matters most and near-bottom on flights.

**Morocco scores respectably by failing only one criterion — the one that matters most.** Cheap,
easy, decent activities, and small end-of-season waves. Included honestly rather than hidden.

## 1. Bali — €1050–1750

**Waves (7/10).** May is dry-season shoulder: clean from first light, swells building, quieter
than peak. April/May named among the best months to learn. Canggu absorbs every level; Uluwatu is
advanced except small days at high tide. Marked down only for crowds — Canggu in May is busy, and
30 beginners in a crowded lineup is a real friction.

**Cost.** Flights **€580–1080**, average ≈ €789 ([Skyscanner](https://www.skyscanner.net/routes/hel/dps/helsinki-vantaa-to-bali-denpasar.html) ·
[KAYAK](https://www.kayak.com/flight-routes/Helsinki-Vantaa-HEL/Denpasar-Bali-Ngurah-Rai-DPS)).
Camps from **€349/week all-inclusive** ([Solid Surf House](https://solidsurfhouse.com/surfcamp/bali/canggu/)),
mid-range **€567–945/week** ([Canggu Surfing price guide](https://canggusurfing.com/canggu-surf-camp-prices-2026-what-you-really-get/) ·
[findsurfcampbali breakdown](https://www.findsurfcampbali.com/blog/surf-camp-costs-in-bali-complete-price-breakdown-2025)).

**Group.** Largest capacity of anywhere on this list. Easiest 30-person booking.

**Activities.** Ubud, Mount Batur sunrise trek, waterfalls, Nusa Penida — all within 2–3 h.

**Watch out.** It's the default answer. Half your group may have already been.

## 2. Lombok — €1200–1800

**Waves (8/10).** Dry season May–Oct, most consistent swells, **2–6 ft**
([Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/surfing-indonesia/lombok-surf/)). **Gerupuk Bay has five
breaks in one bay**, from *Inside Left* (soft, mellow, beginner) to *Outside Gerupuk*
(intermediate+), all a short boat ride from the village
([LMBK guide](https://lmbksurfhouse.com/surf-guide-lombok/inside-gerupuk)). **This is the single
best structural fit for a group with a 0–50 session spread** — everyone surfs an appropriate wave
from one base, no splitting.

Marked down because winds are technically onshore May–Sep; the bay is large and forgiving enough
to stay surfable most mornings, so it's an early-start trip.

**Cost.** Flights via Denpasar then a short hop or fast boat to Lombok — budget **€750–1000**
all-in. Camps: **€990 for 14 nights full board** at
[Surf Camp Lombok](https://www.surfcampindonesia.com/) (≈ €70/night, 26 sessions, video analysis,
theory, yoga, all meals). Also [Nuna Surf](https://nunasurf.com/),
[DHM Surf Camp](https://dhm.co.id/) (flexible dates, mentions accommodating special requests),
[360 Surf Academy](https://www.360surfacademy.com/), [LMBK Surf House](https://lmbksurfhouse.com/),
[Feel Lombok](https://feel-lombok.com/tour/surf-lessons-and-camps/).

**Group (6/10).** The weak point: Lombok camps run **small-group instruction, often max 2 students
per instructor**. Nobody advertises 30-person capacity. Needs a direct conversation —
DHM explicitly mentions special requests.

**Activities (9/10).** Strongest on this list for your 2–4 h rule:
- **Sade / Rembitan Sasak villages** — 7 km from Kuta, traditional weaving, thatched houses
- **Benang Stokal & Benang Kelambu waterfalls** — ~90 min, tours bundle rice terraces and a
  weaving village
- **Gili Islands** (Trawangan, Meno, Air) — snorkelling, turtles
- **Mount Rinjani** — serious trekking for whoever wants it
([Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/top-things-to-do-in-lombok) ·
[Viator day trips](https://www.viator.com/Lombok-tours/Day-Trips-and-Excursions/d22869-g5))

## 3. Nicaragua (Popoyo) — €1200–1900

**Waves (8/10).** April–October prime, SW groundswell **4–8 ft faces**, water **27–29 °C**,
boardshorts only. **May–July is the sweet spot for wanting both power and size and clean surf**
([Nicaragua Surf Camp](https://nicaraguasurfcamp.com/surf-seasons/best-time-to-surf-popoyo-nicaragua/) ·
[Topologica](https://topologica.co/articles/nicaragua-popoyo-surf-guide/)).

Unique advantage: **Lake Nicaragua funnels offshore wind seaward almost daily**, so clean morning
faces are close to guaranteed. Marked down from 9 only because Popoyo is largely **reef, not
sand** — a different proposition at session 3 than Las Flores.

**Cost.** **Cheapest camps on this list** — from **€280/week all-inclusive**; Hide & Seek from
**€420 (~$454) for 7 days** ([Suay Hype guide](https://suayhype.com/surf-camps/nicaragua/popoyo/)).
Flights are the problem: thin routes, budget **€900–1200**.

**Group (8/10).** A real *cluster* of camps, which gives you negotiating leverage:
[Nicawaves](https://www.nicawaves.com/popoyo-nicaragua-surfing-2/popoyo-surf-packages/) (owner is
a two-time US Championships finalist, 20 years local, 10 × 4x4s for spot access) ·
[Popoyo Surf Lodge](https://popoyosurflodge.com/) (country's first) ·
[Las Plumerias](https://www.lasplumerias.com/) (15+ spots) ·
[Popoyo Surf Camp](https://www.popoyosurfcamp.com/surf-packages) (level-split programmes).

**Activities (8/10).** **Cerro Negro volcano boarding** — ride a board down live volcanic ash at
up to 100 kph. That is a direct spiritual successor to the 2026 safari.
([Volcano Day](https://www.volcanodaynicaragua.com/) · [Infinity Travels](https://infinitravels.com/en/tours/cerro-negro-volcano-tour/)).
Also **Ometepe** (twin-volcano island), **Granada** + **Masaya** (walk to the crater rim and look
at magma), **Laguna de Apoyo**.
⚠️ Cerro Negro is near León — **2–3 h each way** from the coast, a full-day commitment.

## 4. El Salvador — €1300–2050

**Waves (9/10) — the best on this list for you.** May–September is the season, and **May–June is
specifically the sweet spot** ([Waverick](https://www.waverick.com/articles/el-salvador/when-to-surf-el-salvador/)).

- **Las Flores** — right-hand point, **~300 m, sand bottom, no rocks or reef**, explicitly rated
  for beginners *and* intermediates ([Salty Souls](https://saltysoulsexperience.com/best-surf-spot-beginner-intermediate-las-flores-el-salvador/) ·
  [Waverick](https://www.waverick.com/articles/el-salvador/surfing-las-flores-el-salvador/))
- **Sunzal** (El Tunco) — longest right-hand point in the area, long and mellow, multiple
  sections, "ideal for building confidence" ([Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/el-salvador-surf/el-tunco-surf/))

This is structurally the Arugam Bay wave you already know works.

**⚠️ There is a real base decision here, and it's a trade-off:**

| Base | Wave | Activities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Las Flores / El Cuco** (east) | Better, quieter, more remote | **Poor** — the Ruta de las Flores is right across the country, 4–5 h away |
| **El Tunco / El Zonte** (La Libertad) | Very good, busier | **Strong** — ~1 h to San Salvador, 1–2 h to the Ruta |

**Cost.** Camps **USD 450–800/week** typical
([Waverick camp reviews](https://www.waverick.com/articles/el-salvador/best-surf-camps-el-salvador/));
premium end: [Casa Las Flores](https://www.booksurfcamps.com/all/d/the-americas-and-caribbean/el-salvador/playa-las-flores)
**€925 / 5 nights**, Laola Surf Camp **€980 / 7 nights**. Standalone lessons USD 25–40, board hire
USD 10–15/day ([Holafly breakdown](https://esim.holafly.com/trip-planning/surf-salvador/)).
Flights **€850–1150**, thin routes.

**Group (6/10).** ⚠️ **[Las Flores Surf Club](https://worldsurfaris.com/surf-trip/las-flores-surf-club-el-salvador/)
caps surf passes at 20 per week** to limit crowding on the wave. **You are ~30.** This is the
single most important question to ask, and it may decide the destination by itself.
Also [Las Flores Resort](https://www.luex.com/surf/places/las-flores-resort-el-salvador.html)
(explicitly serves groups, panga fleet to Punta Mango) and El Zonte options.

**Activities (8/10, from El Tunco).** **Ruta de las Flores** — 32 km of colonial towns and coffee
fincas; the **7 Waterfalls hike** (6 h, views of three volcanoes); **Apaneca canopy zipline**
(longest run 420 m); crater lakes Laguna Verde and Laguna de las Ninfas
([Indie Traveller](https://www.indietraveller.co/ruta-de-las-flores-travel-guide/) ·
[Stingy Nomads](https://stingynomads.com/ruta-de-las-flores-el-salvador/)).

## 5. Costa Rica — €1600–2500

**Waves (8/10).** May opens both wet season and swell season. **Playa Guiones (Nosara)** is
sand-bottomed with multiple peaks for all levels and is the most consistent break in the country;
Santa Teresa is punchier. Intermediates are told to target exactly May–October
([Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/costa-rica-surf/best-time-to-surf-in-costa-rica/)).
Rain is afternoon storms; **wind, not rain, is what degrades surf**.

**Cost — the reason it ranks 5th.** Camps **$800–1500/week**, resorts $1500–2500
([WaveCamps](https://wavecamps.com/central-america/costa-rica/) ·
[Suay Hype Santa Teresa](https://suayhype.com/surf-camps/costa-rica/santa-teresa/)). Individual
lessons $60. Flights average **€759** ([Iberia](https://www.iberia.com/us/cheap-flights/Helsinki-San-Jose-de-Costa-Rica/)).
**Roughly double Nicaragua for a similar wave.**

**Group (7/10).** Santa Teresa has 22+ camps — the most competitive market in the country
— so capacity exists, at a price.

**Activities (9/10).** Arenal volcano, ziplines, wildlife, hot springs. Excellent but expensive.

## 6. Morocco — €700–1200 *(honest inclusion)*

**Waves (4/10) — this is why it's here and not higher.** May is shoulder-to-end of season:
**0.5–1.5 m**, and June–September has high odds of completely flat ocean
([Teddy Pirate](https://www.teddypirate.com/en/taghazout-surf-seasons) ·
[Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/surfing-morocco/taghazout-surf/)). Fine for pure beginners;
too soft for a group that explicitly wants more than beginner waves.

Everything else about it is good — cheapest flights by a wide margin, strong camp infrastructure,
Agadir/Paradise Valley/Atlas day trips. It scores 6.7 by being adequate at everything except the
one criterion carrying 35 % of the weight. You also said it's too close to be interesting.

## 7. Mexico (Pacific) — €1300–2000

**Waves (7/10).** May starts the south-swell season. **La Saladita** is a long mellow left point
running **3–6 ft May–September**, superb for progression
([Surf Without a Car](https://surfwithoutacar.com/la-saladita-surf/)). **Sayulita** is
beginner-friendly and May is end-of-high-season, so cheaper and quieter.

**⚠️ Avoid Puerto Escondido.** Zicatela is among the heaviest beach breaks on earth and is
genuinely dangerous at your level. Nayarit or Guerrero, not Oaxaca.

**Activities (7/10).** Marietas Islands, San Pancho, jungle waterfalls, Sierra Madre.

## 8. Peru (Lobitos / Máncora) — €1300–1950 + wetsuits

**Waves (7/10).** **April–October is the golden window** — S–SW groundswell wrapping the
headlands with **SE offshores dominating mornings**
([Surf Atlas](https://thesurfatlas.com/peru-surf/)). World-class lefts, genuinely uncrowded,
genuinely exotic.

**⚠️ The wetsuit question you raised.** Lobitos water runs **18–22 °C**, dropping to 17–19 °C by
June–July. **This is a wetsuit trip.** Budget **€15–25/person** for rental over the stay, or
€50–80 more if anyone buys — call it **€450–750 extra for a group of 30**, plus the hassle of
sizing 30 wetsuits. Everywhere else on this list is boardshorts-only. That difference is real but
it is *not* what knocks Peru down the list — capacity is.

**Group (4/10) — the actual problem.** The camps that match what you want are *small*.
[Lobitos Surf Natural](https://www.booksurfcamps.com/lobitossurfnatural/6-days-pure-nature-surf-camp-in-northern-peru)
is a **family-run** hostel with meals, accommodation, lessons and gear included and six breaks
nearby — exactly the local operation you described wanting, and almost certainly unable to hold 30.
Also [Wayra Surf Camp](https://www.booksurfcamps.com/wayra-surf-camp/8-days-functional-training-yoga-and-surf-camp-lobitos-peru)
and [Lobitos Surf Academy](https://lobitosperusurf.com/).

## Baggage — a real cost variable

You're right to raise it. On a 9–12 day surf trip everyone needs **23–30 kg checked**, not
carry-on. This changes fare comparisons more than people expect:

- **Turkish, Qatar, Emirates, Finnair** — checked baggage standard in economy. Your 2026 Turkish
  booking didn't even offer a carry-on-only fare.
- **Juneyao** (currently cheapest to Bali, and the airline where Pekka hit the 5-seats-at-a-time
  limit) and other low-cost long-haul — **verify the fare class includes 23 kg.** A €60 headline
  saving can become a €40–80 per-person bag fee, i.e. worse.

**Compare landed cost with bags included, never headline fare.** At 30 people a €50 bag fee is
€1500.

## Two things nobody has raised yet

**1. US transit needs an ESTA.** Many cheap Central America itineraries connect through Miami,
Dallas or Atlanta. Finnish passports need an **ESTA (~$21)** plus approval chasing — for 30
people. 2026 showed what chasing one simple visa approval costs Oona in effort. **Prefer Madrid,
Bogotá or Panama City routings** and treat US-transit fares as more expensive than they look.

**2. May 2027 is bookable now** (~8 months out). Every number above is an estimate; real fares are
one search away and would turn Thursday's argument into arithmetic.
