# The Telegram channels

Four channels, each with one job. The separation is the thing that made 2026 work — the
official feed stayed clean because the chatter had somewhere else to go.

| Channel | Who can post | What belongs there | Who owns it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Admin** (`porsurffi🧊`) | Organizers only | Decisions, budget, vendor talk, agendas | Joel |
| **TIEDOTUS** (announcements) | Organizers only, one-way | Payments, deadlines, schedule, logistics | Joel |
| **LÖRS** (group chat) | Everyone | Free chat, questions, memes | — |
| **Dispatch** (fun/news) | Bot + organizers | Surf report, weather, wildlife, history, trivia | `dispatch` |

The fourth channel is new for 2027. In 2026 this content had nowhere to live, so it either
cluttered TIEDOTUS or never got made.

## Rules that made TIEDOTUS work in 2026

- **One-way.** Participants read it; they reply in LÖRS. No thread noise around a payment
  deadline.
- **One ask per message**, with the deadline as an exact date and time.
- **Payment details repeated in full every time.** People scroll past and lose them.
- **Daily schedule posted the evening before** during the trip itself. Heavy, but it meant
  nobody ever asked "what time tomorrow".

## Bot posting policy

The bot drafts into the admin channel. An organizer reads it and forwards it. The bot never
posts to a participant-facing channel on its own.

The one exception worth considering later is **Dispatch**, where a wrong surf report is
embarrassing rather than expensive. Even there, start with human approval and only loosen it
once the output has been boring and correct for a week.
